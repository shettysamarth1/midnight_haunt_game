How to Play

  • Mouse or Touch: Click and drag / swipe across the screen to slice incoming entities.
  • Wandering Pumpkins (+100 PTS): Slicing them triggers amber bursts.
  • Spectral Wisps (+250 PTS): Freezes all incoming entities in time for 3 seconds.
  • Cursed Skulls: ⚠️ DO NOT SLICE! Costs 1 of your 3 lives. Zero lives ends the run.
  • Witch’s Candy: Slice 3 in a streak to activate Frenzy Harvest with double points and rapid souls.
  • Nightmare Mode: Survive 60 seconds with at least 1 life remaining for Victory!

Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Space or Enter: Start / Resume game
  • P or Esc: Pause game
  • M: Toggle Audio
  • R: Restart round
